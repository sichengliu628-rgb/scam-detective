📱 You Received a Text Message!

Dear Customer,

Your bank account has been frozen!
Please click the link below immediately to verify your account.

www.bank-verify.example

Is this a scam or a safe message?

Why is this a scam?

🚨 It creates a sense of urgency.

🔗 It asks you to click an unfamiliar link.

⚠️ The message may be impersonating your bank.

🛡️ Safety Tip

Never click suspicious links. Contact your bank using its official phone number or mobile app.
